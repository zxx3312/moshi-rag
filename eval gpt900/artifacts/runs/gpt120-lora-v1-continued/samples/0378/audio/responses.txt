Maria Sophia of Neuburg was queen of Portugal as the wife of King Peter II. Peter II of Portugal was the spouse of Maria Sophia of Neuburg. Luisa de Guzmán was the mother of Peter II of Portugal, making her Maria Sophia of Neuburg’s mother-in-law.
